Where the 680 examples came from

500 source interviews → 273 structural candidates → 237 interviews contributing retained examples

The structural inventory excludes 227 interviews. Direct screening covers all 273 candidates: 266 received continuation review, while seven were excluded from opening inspection. The fixed 1,186 candidate points and 72 additionally reviewed targets are all accounted for.

680 retained / 541 reference-only

1,221 decision points received individual judgments. Another 37 candidates were excluded with their opening-only interviews; their target behavior was not assessed.

Questions depending on unseen books, statistics, images or interview preparation remain reference-only when the available input cannot support them. Short clarifications can be restored with a recorded reason.

Real wording, explicit interpretation

The response is original transcript text. The Mission, behavior category and keep/reference judgment are assistant-authored interpretations. Inferred requirements are optional and unconditional because authentic requiredness and branching are unavailable.

No historical Evidence State, tool action, A/B preference or completion decision is fabricated.

Collection → opening guide → decision point

  1. Account for the complete pinned 500-interview archive and freeze whole-conversation groups before annotation.
  2. Read only the fixed opening, then save an inferred questionnaire and Mission with exact opening evidence.
  3. Read the continuation directly; retain original next turns supported by the preceding dialogue and fixed Mission. Preserve reasons for reference-only and excluded records.
  4. Export Mission plus complete prior dialogue as the prompt and the unchanged human next turn as completion. No authoritative Harness State or historical tool actions are invented.
  5. Verify source spans, prefix boundaries and groups; tokenize and load every row with the real pinned template. Mask everything except original target wording.

The source is edited published news-interview text from the ConvoKit NewsInterview subset. Full-interview summaries, outlines and information-item annotations are excluded from model-visible inputs. Guide records disclose any earlier pilot exposure.

Usable data format, verified locally

All 680 rows passed original-target and strictly prior-prefix checks. 11,802 original utterance mappings were verified, 266 Missions were recompiled, and all 9 frozen Harness production files retained their hashes.

The actual pinned Qwen3.5 tokenizer and official chat template processed every row. Maximum complete sequence length is 5,046 tokens under a 16,384-token limit: zero truncation and zero rejection. A CPU loader materialized 171 padded batches and checked every label position. Only original target wording is supervised; prompts, history, template prefixes, EOS and padding are masked.

A deterministic rebuild reproduced 19 content artifacts byte-for-byte. No model weights, forward/backward pass or optimizer were used. There is no separate semantic reviewer or framework training-loop result.

What remains outside this result

  • One edited journalistic source batch; transfer to live user interviews is untested.
  • Assistant curation, with no human A/B preferences or independent semantic agreement estimate.
  • Missions are inferred from openings, not recovered original questionnaires. Requiredness and branches are unknown.
  • Source speaker labels can be wrong; lexical/event grouping may miss aliases or shared events. Hosts overlap across splits; pretraining contamination is unknown.
  • No qualified defer/respect-boundary examples. Five natural farewells are not native completion or user-withdrawal labels.
  • No model training, reward model, long-horizon optimization or interviewing-quality improvement is established.
