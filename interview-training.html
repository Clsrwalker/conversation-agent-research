What changed, and what stayed matched

The question is whether one supervised epoch improves prediction and generation of interview continuations. The baseline is the same frozen Qwen3.5-9B model with its adapter disabled. The candidate enables the trained LoRA adapter; this is a weight-training comparison, not evidence that a Harness mechanism improved.

All checkpoints score the same 404 development examples, 202 conversation groups and 12,515 supervised target tokens. IDs, order, source, group, profile and target-token counts match. Step 218 was selected over step 109 by the predefined mean per-example development negative log likelihood (NLL). Lower NLL means better prediction of the recorded reference wording.

The corpus combines quoted-context continuations with native Harness prompts. The native profile supervises only the human speech inside message; the JSON wrapper, action decision and EOS receive no loss. Its 475 train examples and 55 dev examples do not teach a complete Actor action contract.

Exploratory uncertainty and weighting

Paired source-stratified cluster bootstrap, 2,000 replicates with seed 20261116: the 95% interval for the step-218 minus base per-example NLL difference is [-0.6993, -0.6051]; for step 218 minus step 109 it is [-0.0173, -0.0094]. Negative differences favor the later checkpoint.

These are descriptive intervals on the development set used for selection, not independent test estimates. UK supplies 160 of 202 groups; Fed has only one, Bath two and RRING three. The intervals do not correct for checkpoint selection or multiple diagnostic slices.

Shorter replies and fewer capped outputs are measurable changes, but do not establish relevance, completeness or better interviewing. A repeated eight-token sequence means an eight-token window occurs at least three times in one output. Repeated-output counts include all cases sharing the same nonempty output within an arm.

Native schema validity falls from 3/55 to 0/55 under free decoding. Neither arm demonstrates a reliable native Actor interface. Because this training masked the wrapper and decisions, the result does not measure success under structured decoding, and it does not establish a causal mechanism for the failures.

Completed recovery, preserved failures

Earlier attempts were interrupted by a Windows restart and native-process / host instability. One parent run reached step 218 but crashed during final development scoring, after 225 of 404 examples. The final run resumed from the step-200 recovery checkpoint, completed the remaining updates, evaluated all 404 dev examples and saved the selected step-218 adapter. Those abandoned attempts are retained locally; the earlier host instability remains unresolved.

The generation comparison also retained an initial backend-policy ordering error, then completed the locked comparison. Completion and artifact consistency establish execution, not successful interview answers.

What this result supports

The demonstrated finding is improved development-reference prediction with diminishing late gains, alongside mixed free-generation diagnostics. The trained checkpoint is not promoted into the frozen Harness. An independent test evaluation and blinded answer-quality review are still needed before claiming improved interviewing.

  • Development split used for checkpoint selection; not an independent test-set estimate. No new model inference or training.
  • NLL measures prediction of observed reference speech, not interview decision quality or human preference.
  • Source-stratified cluster bootstrap retains paired rows; UK supplies 160/202 groups. Fed has one group; its stratum interval is omitted, while overall intervals condition on that observed group. Bath has two groups and RRING three.
  • Bootstrap intervals are descriptive reanalysis of selected development results and do not adjust for checkpoint selection or multiple diagnostic slices.
  • Native speech-only training masked the JSON wrapper, decisions and EOS. Free greedy JSON validity is 3/55 before and 0/55 after; structured decoding and online Harness behavior are untested.
  • Shorter outputs and fewer capped outputs do not alone prove better answers; repeated outputs and repeated eight-grams increased.
  • No 381-case test evaluation or human A/B ratings; no production Harness promotion.
