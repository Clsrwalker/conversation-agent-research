What failed?

  • Omitted requirements27 / 48
  • Stale or lost state3 / 48
  • Execution errors16 / 48
  • False completion2 / 48

Runs can have several errors. Execution errors include rejected tool actions (11 / 48 runs) and reviewed record or rating errors. These observations do not prove an internal memory or intent failure.

  1. Saved-state claims exceed the evidence

    2 runs claim a saved assessment after failed saves, without a successful commit. Some claims follow scripted fallback replies; they remain false at the time they are delivered.

    2 supporting runs ↗
  2. Assessment records lose evidence distinctions

    6 runs persist incorrect assistance flags or answer-source links. Correct answer text alone does not establish an accurate trainee assessment.

    6 supporting runs ↗
  3. Receipt format affects the headline score

    5 runs fail only because a truthful confirmation omits the literal receipt ID. Accepting those confirmations changes strict completion from 10/42 to 15/42. This is a scoring sensitivity, not a harness improvement.

    5 supporting runs ↗

Business persistence recovered: 7 / 8 encountered injected failures; this does not imply full task success. 8 / 12 assigned failure hooks were reached.

How to read this result

Six source dialogues were selected from Taskmaster automotive conversations and MetaLWOz phone-plan conversations. Cases and live replies are scripted adaptations; ASE and AUR inform the automotive task scope.

One model, six development families, no independent domain-expert review. Business state is checked directly; semantic scores use provisional rubrics and AI-assisted trace review. No state, verification or async intervention has been compared yet.
