Original guide → Mission → observed decision

  1. Preserve source files, download receipts, attribution and hashes. Freeze whole-interview splits and guide mappings before reading transcript bodies.
  2. Map two Bath guides and the RRING protocol to 41 information needs. Compile directly authored needs with the existing Mission compiler; no annotation model is called.
  3. Read every transcript, resolve supported speaker mappings and review each candidate. Keep the prior conversation and the original human interviewer’s next-turn wording, with exact source locations.
  4. Keep uncertainty visible. Unsupported assumptions, missing material, mixed speakers and unsuitable follow-ups remain reference records with local reasons.
  5. Freeze source inputs, reviews and code; verify source reconstruction, strictly prior histories, split boundaries and deterministic rebuilding.

Historical requiredness and native completion are unavailable. Compiled needs are optional; RRING’s original topic order and conditional follow-ups are recorded separately as protocol constraints. No historical Evidence State or tool action is invented.

Source defects remain part of the record

Mixed-speaker sections prevent retention when their meaning affects the prior context. Documented label reversals and continuation paragraphs are explicitly mapped, without claiming audio verification. Seventy-three marked backchannels or nonverbal asides in one transcript remain separate, source-linked annotations with unknown speakers.

Three legacy Word documents were converted with source and output hashes retained. Their text was checked against Word’s extracted content, ignoring whitespace and cell markers. Missing images, omitted passages and unseen interview preparation are not reconstructed.

What this release does not establish

  • One assistant curated the material; there is no independent semantic agreement estimate or human A/B preference study.
  • These are published transcripts, potentially edited or translated; source role decisions have not been checked against audio.
  • Shared guides and interviewers limit split independence. Sparse deferral, correction and refusal examples do not establish broad coverage.
  • No authoritative historical Evidence State, native completion decision or Harness session replay is supplied.
  • The new batch has not undergone model tokenizer-budget or training-loader validation. It is a curated data release, not a verified training package.
  • No training, reward model, long-horizon optimization or interviewing-quality improvement has been measured.

Meeting progress: Stage 3 collection and refinement are complete for this batch. Human A/B calibration, reward modeling and subsequent training have not been performed in this work.
